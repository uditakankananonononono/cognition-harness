# Originality exploration: split-before-discard with retained resurrection evidence

Hypothesis proposal, not novelty or capability verdict. No experiment scored, no evaluator final data accessed, no earlier freeze changed.

## Known prior, not ours

- Observational-equivalence pruning and counterexample refinement overlap directly with2017SYNGAR: https://arxiv.org/html/1710.07740 . The paper describes abstract-semantics grouping and refinement of spurious candidates.
-2025counterexample-guided e-graphs extend observational equivalence with counterexamples: https://www.pure.ed.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/564446902/FrankelEtalSMT2025Syntax-GuidedSynthesis.pdf . This defeats any claim we invented splitting equivalence classes from extra observations.
-1992query-by-committee selects queries by maximum disagreement: https://mlanthology.org/colt/1992/seung1992colt-query/ . Choosing a probe by candidate disagreement is established.
-Metamorphic testing derives further tests and can avoid a full oracle: https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.12543 . The archive upload2020is distinct from its original technical-report identifierHKUST-CS98-01.
-Observational similarity is another nearby relaxation: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3622830 . Page availability/verification caveat retained in research ledger.
-Equivalence reduction is another nearby synthesis approach: https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10100598 . Full methods comparison still needed before a narrower novelty verdict.

No six-paper search proves absence of other prior. Core ingredients are known; all-world originality is UNESTABLISHED.

## Candidate combination to investigate

When two generated programs share a development-output vector, do not erase the second source. Retain bounded source hashes and a resurrection lineage. Select an additional probe from a separately preregistered public-domain pool by maximum disagreement, WITHOUT expected answers or final evaluator-case access. Split candidate buckets by outputs and keep one representative per resulting bucket. Probe outputs expose disagreement, not which program is correct. A separate known invariant/reference must judge correctness before any performance claim.

Candidate engineering delta: a finite byte-budgeted recovery ledger ties every discarded source hash to the exact pruning decision, pool/version, probe cost and later split/resurrection event. Probe selection must have explicit evaluator-custody exclusion and receipt accounting. This is a proposed integration of known techniques, not a novel synthesis theorem or proof of better generalization.

## Frozen experiment needed before scoring

Compare first-vector representative vs fixed diversity retention vs disagreement-resurrection with the SAME grammar, source budget, probe domain and total execution budget. Count pool scans, scalar probes, worker evaluations and retained bytes. Independent evaluator keeps final tasks/answers; do not reuse the spent M7/M8 final sets as fresh. Freeze source/tie rules/screening caps and novelty question before evaluating. Include negative cases where candidates agree on every available probe and where disagreement protects a worse candidate. Ship nulls and cap exhaustion. Descriptive performance only until sufficiently varied independent tasks exist.

No LLM/AGI/arbitrary invention, model/runtime superiority, owner authorization or activation claim. An originality aspiration does not supply evidence of novelty. Next prototype can build the deterministic ledger/split mechanics and verify invariants, not claim a learned-method win.

## Prototype mechanics only

Eight new unit tests pass for maximum-disagreement/tie choice, typed bool/int partition, no-disagreement collapse, cap lineage, missing/unequal vectors and unsupported-output refusal. These do NOT test generalization, correctness of synthesized programs, novelty, byte-budget implementation, resurrection itself or superiority. Current prototype stores lineage IDs/groups, not a durable authenticated byte-budget ledger. Float/dict outputs intentionally outside the small prototype domain. Input vectors are externally evaluated, not supplied by a trusted oracle here. No benchmark score or benefit claim.
